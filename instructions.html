Welcome to GoDik

Your Hong Kong Taxi Companion

Search any destination in English or Chinese, show a large bilingual flashcard to the driver, and speak natural Cantonese in one tap.

How it works
1
Search or Pick a Destination
Type any building, street, hotel, or estate in English or 中文. You can also pick from Recent, Saved, Popular, and more.
2
Show the Card or Speak Cantonese
Tap anywhere on the driver card to go fullscreen, or tap 🔊 Speak Cantonese to play natural Cantonese audio.
3
Add ons & Directions
If you want to specify a tunnel or payment option, you can click Add ons to add more details to the taxi card, open Google Maps, or load live GPS directions on the map.
📱 Add to iPhone Home Screen

Open GoDik in Safari, tap the Share button, and choose Add to Home Screen to install it as a standalone app with offline support for your saved and popular destinations.
